# Bio

I'm an Associate Professor of Computer Science at Nanjing University, where I also received my BS in 2011 and PhD in 2017 (advisors: [Chang Xu](https://cs.nju.edu.cn/changxu/), [Xiaoxing Ma](https://ics.nju.edu.cn/~xxm/), and [Jian Lyu](https://cs.nju.edu.cn/58/2a/c2639a153642/page.htm)). In 2015, I spent a memorable half-year at the Ohio State, working with [Feng Qin](https://u.osu.edu/fengqin/) and happily becoming a Buckeye.

I'm now working on *agentic AI systems and technologies behind test-time scaling*, with general intelligence as the broader horizon. Before the LLM era, I worked on systems software and software automation: operating system kernels, compilers, virtual machines, middleware, and the machinery through which software constructs and transforms itself.

In Fall 2026, I will launch *[Generative Software Engineering](https://jyywiki.cn/GSE/2026/)*, to pursuit the understanding of how software is built, and then rebuilding the way we build it. I'll discontinue the *Operating Systems* and Project-N (proudly with Zihao Yu), which grew beyond the classroom, receiving more than 5,000,000 views, and supported by the *Hitchhiker’s Guide to OS* [paper](https://www.usenix.org/conference/atc23/presentation/jiang-yanyan).


Competitive programming was once a part of my life. I won first prizes in regional contests, placed second in the 2012 Tencent Hackathon, and competed in the 2009 ACM-ICPC World Finals. I later spent 20 years setting problems for the Jiangsu Provincial Programming Competition, eventually serving as technical committee chair (now retired), and served for three years on the TC of the Asia-Pacific Informatics Olympiad.
